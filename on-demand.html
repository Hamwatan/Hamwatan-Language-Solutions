How a project works

  1. You describe the scope, languages, deadline and files.
  2. We confirm the scope and quote in writing.
  3. You approve the quote, and we agree delivery terms.
  4. We deliver the work and confirm it meets the agreed standards.

Questions

What is an on-demand project?

A single defined piece of work, such as dubbing a video or running a training workshop. You order it when you need it, and it is priced and scoped on its own.

How is the price set?

Each service shows its own estimate. Final prices are confirmed in writing after we review the scope, before any payment.
