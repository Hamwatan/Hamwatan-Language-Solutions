How booking works

  1. Choose the setting, mode, language, date, start time and length.
  2. We confirm that an interpreter is available and matched to your need.
  3. Share the agenda or form in advance, without medical details in the booking form.
  4. The interpreter joins, interprets accurately and completely, and stays neutral.
  5. We send a short session summary if you request one.

Our standards

  • Accurate and complete interpreting, with plain-language explanations of technical terms.
  • Neutral conduct. Interpreters do not advocate for any party.
  • Confidentiality agreements signed before every assignment.
  • Professional ethics and HIPAA-aware practice for healthcare work.

Questions

How much notice do you need?

We recommend at least 48 hours for on-site interpreting. Video and phone interpreting can often be arranged the same day, depending on availability.

Do you match interpreters by dialect?

Yes. We match each booking to an interpreter who speaks the dialect your client uses and understands the setting, where possible.

Are your interpreters confidential?

Yes. Every interpreter signs a confidentiality agreement before work starts and must keep all client information private.

Can I include medical details in the booking form?

Please do not. Share the setting, language, date, and length. Share sensitive details through a secure channel after booking.
