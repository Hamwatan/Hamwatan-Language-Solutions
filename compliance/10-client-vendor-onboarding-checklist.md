# Client and Vendor Onboarding Checklist

Version 1.0 | Owner: [OWNER NAME] | Effective: [DATE]

This checklist is for organizations (hospitals, schools, law firms, government programs, and employers) that want to set up a contract with Hamwatan. Individuals who order a single service do not need this step.

## 1. Before we begin
- [ ] Written request describing the organization, languages, service types, expected monthly volume, and locations.
- [ ] Name and contact details of the client's coordinator and billing contact.

## 2. Documents the organization may need from Hamwatan
- [ ] Certificate of insurance (general liability and professional liability), with limits.
- [ ] IRS Form W-9 for Hamwatan.
- [ ] Business registration certificate from the Virginia State Corporation Commission, once formed.
- [ ] Federal UEI and SAM.gov registration confirmation, where the client is a federal agency or contractor.
- [ ] eVA vendor registration number, where the client is a Virginia state agency.
- [ ] Copies of this compliance document set on request (information security, retention schedule, quality manual, complaints procedure).
- [ ] Confidentiality agreement signed by Hamwatan and by each assigned linguist.

## 3. Documents the client may ask Hamwatan to sign
- [ ] Vendor or referral agreement.
- [ ] Non-disclosure agreement, if the client uses its own form.
- [ ] Business Associate Agreement, for any protected health information (document 04).
- [ ] Background check or security clearance where the work requires it.
- [ ] Sign-in or time-tracking forms for on-site work.
- [ ] Insurance and indemnity clauses required by the client.

## 4. Setup
- [ ] Agree the scope, rates, invoicing cycle, and payment terms in writing.
- [ ] Agree the scheduling method and the named coordinator on each side.
- [ ] Agree the secure file transfer method before any documents are shared.
- [ ] Set up the assigned linguist roster and confirm each has signed the confidentiality agreement.
- [ ] Agree the complaints contact and the escalation path.

## 5. First-order review
- [ ] Review the first project against the quality standard.
- [ ] Agree any terminology glossary.
- [ ] Schedule a check-in after 30 days.

## 6. Ongoing
- [ ] Monthly invoice and volume summary.
- [ ] Quarterly review of quality, complaints, and volume.
- [ ] Annual review of contract terms and this checklist.
