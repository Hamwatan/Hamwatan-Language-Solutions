## Before you send any document to a client
- Confirm the legal entity name, address, and registration number. Until the LLC is formed, do not describe the company as an LLC.
- Confirm insurance is in force and the certificate matches the stated limits.
- Confirm each named person has actually signed the relevant agreement.
- Replace every bracketed placeholder, such as [COMPANY LEGAL NAME].
- Date the document and keep a copy of the version sent.

## Ownership
The Owner is responsible for keeping these documents current. Review them at least once a year, and after any change in services, systems, or law.
