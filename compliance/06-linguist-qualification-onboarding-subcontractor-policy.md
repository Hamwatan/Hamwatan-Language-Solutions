# Linguist Qualification, Onboarding and Subcontractor Policy

Version 1.0 | Owner: [OWNER NAME] | Effective: [DATE]

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets the minimum standard for every translator, interpreter, reviewer, and other linguist who works for Hamwatan, and how they are approved and kept qualified.

## 2. Minimum qualifications
A linguist must meet all of these:
- Native or near-native fluency in the source and target languages, including the dialect offered (Afghan Dari, Afghan Pashto, Iranian Farsi, or other).
- Demonstrated writing and reading skill in English.
- At least two years of relevant professional experience, or an equivalent combination of education and experience reviewed by the Owner.
- Passing result on the Company's timed sample test.
- Valid identity verification.

Additional credentials are recorded but are not always required. Examples: ATA certification, a state court registration, CCHI or NBCMI certification, or a 40-hour medical interpreting course.

## 3. Verification
- The Owner checks credentials against the issuing body where a check is possible.
- At least two references are contacted for linguists who will work on certified or legal work.
- Background checks are required for work in schools, courts, law enforcement, and some federal programs, as the client requires.

## 4. Onboarding steps
1. Application with languages, dialects, credentials, experience, and availability.
2. Credential and reference checks.
3. Sample test.
4. Signing the Confidentiality Agreement (document 03) before any paid work.
5. Signing the independent contractor agreement and providing tax forms (for example, IRS Form W-9).
6. Training on confidentiality, data handling, and HIPAA basics (document 12 records completion).
7. Access to the secure work environment, granted only after steps 4 to 6.

## 5. Eligibility to work in the United States
Linguists must be based in the United States, as the Company's current policy states. The Company keeps the records its own contracts require.

## 6. Ongoing qualification
- A quality check is recorded for each linguist after an assignment that is reviewed.
- Linguists must tell the Company within 10 business days about any change in credentials, a complaint against them, or a conflict of interest.
- Refresher training is required once a year.
- Performance below standard leads to coaching, reassignment, or removal from the roster. The reason is recorded.

## 7. Subcontracting
- The Company does not subcontract a whole project to another agency without written client approval.
- Any subcontractor follows the same confidentiality and quality requirements and signs the same agreements.
- The client is told in the quote if work will be done by a subcontractor.

## 8. Conflicts of interest
A linguist does not work on a matter in which they, a family member, or an organization they belong to has an interest, or where they know a party. The linguist declares this before the assignment.

## 9. Removal
A linguist is removed from the roster for a confidentiality breach, falsified credentials, or repeated serious quality failures. Removal is recorded and the linguist's access is ended the same day.
