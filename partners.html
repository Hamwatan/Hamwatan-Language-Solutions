How to start

  1. Pick your channel

    Drop us a line by email, type into our contact form, ring our toll-free line, post a letter to our box, or send it by fax. Whichever way you choose, we will answer.

  2. Minimum requirements

    A minimum of 10 hours per month, one named contact, a written agreement, and the vendor forms, insurance and checks your sector requires.

  3. Agreement

    We agree scope, rates, confidentiality and invoicing in writing. You can use your own vendor templates.

  4. Onboarding

    We complete vendor forms, insurance and background checks, then set up scheduling and a named contact.

Questions

What do organizations get from a partnership?

Priority scheduling, a named coordinator, written confidentiality and quality standards, predictable pricing, and one monthly invoice.

Do partners need to sign anything first?

Yes. Partnerships are agreed in writing. Depending on the organization, that includes a referral or service agreement, vendor registration, background checks and, for health data, a business associate agreement.
