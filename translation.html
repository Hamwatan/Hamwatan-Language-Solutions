What "certified" means

  • A human translator translates the document word for word in meaning.
  • A second linguist checks names, numbers, dates, seals and stamps.
  • Shamsi (Solar Hijri) dates are converted to the Gregorian calendar and checked twice.
  • A signed certificate of accuracy is attached to the translation.

Notarization and apostille

Notarization adds a notary's confirmation of who signed the accuracy statement. It does not verify the translation itself. An apostille is a certificate from the Virginia Secretary of the Commonwealth, needed when a document is used in another country.

Questions

Do you translate Afghan Dari and Iranian Farsi differently?

Yes. Each document goes to a translator who knows its variety. Afghan documents go to Afghan Dari or Pashto specialists, because names, dates, seals and official terms follow Afghan conventions.

Will USCIS accept your translations?

Our certified translations include a signed statement that the translator is competent and the translation is complete and accurate. The final decision belongs to the receiving agency.

What if my photos are blurry?

Send the clearest photos you can. If any part cannot be read, we ask for a better copy before we start, so nothing is guessed.

My tazkira shows my age instead of a full birth date. What do you do?

We translate exactly what the document says and never add a date that is not in it.
