# Interpreter Code of Conduct

Version 1.0 | Owner: [OWNER NAME] | Effective: [DATE]

Every interpreter working through Hamwatan agrees to follow this code. It is based on common professional standards for medical, legal, and community interpreters, including the principles of accuracy, impartiality, confidentiality, and role clarity.

## 1. Accuracy and completeness
- Interpret everything that is said, including tone and meaning, without adding, omitting, or editing content.
- Ask for clarification when a term is unclear, rather than guessing.
- Use the first person when the speaker does, and say when a message is being repeated or clarified.

## 2. Impartiality
- Do not give advice, opinions, or personal views.
- Do not take sides or act as an advocate for the client, the provider, the school, or the agency.
- Declare any conflict of interest before the session starts.

## 3. Confidentiality
- Keep everything heard in the session private, including after the session ends.
- Do not discuss sessions on social media, in messaging apps, or with people outside the assignment.
- Do not record a session unless the Company, the organization, and all parties have agreed in writing.

## 4. Role boundaries
- Interpret only. Do not counsel, diagnose, translate beyond the session, or make decisions for anyone.
- If asked to do something outside interpreting, explain your role and ask the coordinator for guidance.

## 5. Professional conduct
- Arrive on time, prepared, and dressed appropriately for the setting.
- Use the agreed technology and test it in advance for video or phone sessions.
- Treat every person with respect, regardless of background, dialect, gender, religion, or disability.

## 6. Wellbeing and capacity
- Tell the coordinator if you cannot work safely (illness, fatigue, or a conflict). Do not start a session you cannot complete accurately.
- Take breaks in long sessions and ask for a team interpreter where the setting requires it.

## 7. Health information
When a session involves health information, follow the HIPAA procedures in the Information Security Policy and the Business Associate Agreement. Do not take notes that leave the secure environment.

## 8. Reporting
Report any safety concern, suspected abuse, threat, or confidentiality breach to the coordinator at once. Where the law requires a mandatory report, follow the law and inform the coordinator.

## 9. Acknowledgment
By working for Hamwatan, the interpreter agrees to this code. Breach of this code can lead to removal from the roster.
