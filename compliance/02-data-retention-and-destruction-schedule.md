# Data Retention and Destruction Schedule

Version 1.0 | Owner: [OWNER NAME] | Effective: [DATE]

## 1. Purpose
This schedule states how long each type of information is kept and how it is destroyed, so that information is not kept longer than needed.

## 2. Schedule

| Information | Retention period | Method at end of period |
|---|---|---|
| Uploaded client documents | 90 days after the project closes, unless the client asks for a shorter or longer period in writing | Secure deletion from storage, including backups within their cycle |
| Translations delivered to the client | 90 days after the project closes, unless the client asks otherwise | Secure deletion |
| Interpreting session notes and summaries | 90 days after the session | Secure deletion |
| Order records (invoice, amount, dates, service) | As required by tax and accounting rules (generally at least 3 to 7 years) | Deletion after the period |
| Payment records | Held by the payment provider and as required by tax rules | Per provider and tax rules |
| Application and linguist records | Two years after the last activity, or as the law requires | Secure deletion |
| Signed NDAs and BAAs | Duration of the relationship plus six years | Secure deletion |
| Complaints and corrections records | Three years after closure | Secure deletion |
| Website form submissions | 12 months unless they become an order | Secure deletion |
| Email correspondence about a project | Same as documents for that project | Secure deletion |

## 3. Legal holds
If a dispute, audit, or legal request is known or expected, deletion stops for the affected records until the matter is closed. The Owner records the hold in writing.

## 4. Client requests
Clients may ask earlier deletion or a longer retention period in writing. Requests are completed within 30 days, unless a legal hold or tax rule applies, and the client is told which applies.

## 5. Destruction standards
- Electronic: delete from the working folder, the trash, and the sync or backup copy within the backup cycle.
- Paper: cross-cut shred. Client originals held under section 3 of the Information Security Policy are returned, not destroyed.

## 6. Records of destruction
The Owner keeps a short log per batch: date, record type, count, and method. The log does not contain client content.
