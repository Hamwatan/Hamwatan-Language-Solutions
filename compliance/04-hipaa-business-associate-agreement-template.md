# HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (Template)

This Business Associate Agreement ("BAA") is made on [DATE] between [COVERED ENTITY LEGAL NAME] ("Covered Entity") and Hamwatan Language Solutions, [COMPANY LEGAL NAME AND ADDRESS] ("Business Associate").

## 1. Background
The Covered Entity is a health care provider, health plan, or health care clearinghouse subject to the HIPAA Privacy and Security Rules (45 CFR Parts 160 and 164). The Business Associate provides interpreting and translation services that involve Protected Health Information ("PHI"). This Agreement meets the requirements of 45 CFR 164.504(e) and 164.314.

## 2. Definitions
Terms not defined here have the meanings in the HIPAA Rules. "PHI" means individually identifiable health information the Business Associate creates, receives, maintains, or transmits for the Covered Entity.

## 3. Permitted uses and disclosures
The Business Associate may use and disclose PHI only to perform the interpreting and translation services in the Covered Entity's written orders, or as required by law. It will not use or disclose PHI in a way the Covered Entity could not.

## 4. Safeguards
The Business Associate will use appropriate administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to prevent use or disclosure of PHI other than as this Agreement allows, and will comply with the HIPAA Security Rule for electronic PHI. Safeguards include encrypted transmission, access limited to assigned staff and linguists, multi-factor authentication, and private storage.

## 5. Subcontractors
The Business Associate will require each linguist or subcontractor that handles PHI to agree in writing to the same restrictions and conditions, under 45 CFR 164.502(e)(1)(ii) and 164.308(b)(2).

## 6. Reporting
The Business Associate will report to the Covered Entity any use or disclosure of PHI not provided for by this Agreement, and any security incident, without unreasonable delay and within [FIVE] business days of discovery. The report includes the information the Covered Entity needs to meet its breach notification obligations.

## 7. Access, amendment and accounting
Where PHI is held in a designated record set, the Business Associate will make it available for access, amendment, and accounting of disclosures as required by 45 CFR 164.524, 164.526, and 164.528, within the timeframes the Covered Entity requires.

## 8. Books and records
The Business Associate will make its internal practices, books, and records relating to PHI available to the Secretary of Health and Human Services for determining the Covered Entity's compliance.

## 9. Return or destruction
At termination, the Business Associate will return or destroy all PHI it holds, including copies held by subcontractors, and certify this in writing. If return or destruction is not feasible, it will extend these protections and limit further uses to the purposes that make return or destruction infeasible.

## 10. Term and termination
This Agreement lasts as long as the Business Associate holds PHI for the Covered Entity. The Covered Entity may terminate it if the Business Associate materially breaches it and does not cure the breach within [30] days.

## 11. Miscellaneous
This Agreement is governed by federal HIPAA law and the laws of the Commonwealth of Virginia where not preempted. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.
