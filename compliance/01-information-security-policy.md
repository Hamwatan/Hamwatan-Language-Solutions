# Information Security Policy

Version 1.0 | Owner: [OWNER NAME], Owner | Effective: [DATE]

## 1. Purpose
This policy protects the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of client information, including documents, recordings, personal data, and health information, that Hamwatan Language Solutions ("Hamwatan") receives or creates.

## 2. Scope
It applies to the owner, staff, subcontracted linguists and interpreters, and any system that stores or transmits client information, including email, file storage, the website forms, payment tools, and phones or video tools used for interpreting.

## 3. Principles
- Collect only the information needed for the job.
- Share it only with people who need it for the job.
- Keep it no longer than the Data Retention Schedule allows.
- Protect it in transit and at rest.

## 4. Access control
- Each person has a named account. No shared logins.
- Access to client folders is limited to the staff member and linguist assigned to the order.
- Multi-factor authentication is required on email, file storage, payment, and website administration accounts.
- Access is removed within one business day when a person leaves or a project closes.
- Access reviews are done every six months.

## 5. Secure transfer and storage
- Clients upload documents only through the secure upload form or an approved secure file-sharing link. Documents are not sent by ordinary email or WhatsApp.
- Files are stored in private folders. Public links and "anyone with the link" sharing are disabled.
- Devices used for client work use full-disk encryption, a screen lock, and current security updates.
- Paper originals are scanned into a private folder and returned by tracked mail when they must be held at all.

## 6. Sensitive information
- Clients are told not to send Social Security numbers, bank details, or identity numbers unless the translation requires them.
- Unneeded sensitive data found in a file is deleted and the client is told.
- Protected health information is handled only under a signed Business Associate Agreement (see document 04).

## 7. Staff and linguist obligations
- Every person signs a confidentiality agreement before access.
- Each person completes security and confidentiality training at onboarding and once a year.
- Personal devices are not used for client work unless approved in writing and secured under section 5.

## 8. Incidents
Any suspected loss, exposure, or misdirected file is reported to the Owner immediately and handled under the Incident Response Procedure (document 08).

## 9. Vendors
Each software or service provider that stores or processes client data must have written terms that protect the data. The Owner keeps a list of these providers.

## 10. Review
This policy is reviewed every 12 months and after any significant incident or change in systems.
