# Linguist Confidentiality Agreement (Non-Disclosure Agreement)

This Agreement is made on [DATE] between Hamwatan Language Solutions, [COMPANY LEGAL NAME AND ADDRESS] ("Company"), and [LINGUIST FULL LEGAL NAME], [ADDRESS] ("Linguist").

## 1. Purpose
The Company engages the Linguist to translate, interpret, review, or otherwise work on client materials. This Agreement sets the terms for handling that information.

## 2. Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means all client documents, recordings, names, personal data, health information, case details, pricing, and any other information the Linguist receives through the Company, in any form.

## 3. Obligations
The Linguist agrees to:
a. use Confidential Information only to perform the assigned work;
b. not copy, store, forward, or disclose it, except as the assigned work requires;
c. store any working files only in the secure environment the Company provides or approves;
d. delete all copies, including working files and any personal device copies, when the assignment ends and confirm deletion in writing if asked;
e. report any suspected loss or exposure to the Company within 24 hours;
f. not use personal email, messaging apps, cloud storage, or personal devices for client work unless the Company approves it in writing;
g. not discuss client matters with anyone outside the assignment, including family, friends, or social media.

## 4. Health information
Where an assignment involves protected health information, the Linguist agrees to follow the Company's HIPAA training and procedures and to report any incident immediately.

## 5. Exceptions
Confidentiality does not cover information that is publicly available without breach of this Agreement, or disclosure the law requires. In that case the Linguist notifies the Company first, where legally permitted.

## 6. Independent contractor
The Linguist is an independent contractor unless the parties agree otherwise in writing. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship.

## 7. Duration
Obligations under this Agreement continue for [FIVE] years after the last assignment, and for as long as the information remains confidential where it is a trade secret or personal health information.

## 8. Return and deletion
On request or at the end of any assignment, the Linguist returns or deletes all Confidential Information and confirms this in writing.

## 9. Remedies
The Linguist acknowledges that a breach may cause harm that money alone cannot repair. The Company may seek injunctive relief in addition to other remedies available by law.

## 10. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Virginia. Disputes are heard in the state or federal courts located in Virginia.

## 11. Entire agreement
This Agreement is the entire agreement on its subject matter and may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.
