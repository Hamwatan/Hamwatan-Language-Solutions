# Complaints and Corrections Procedure

Version 1.0 | Owner: [OWNER NAME] | Effective: [DATE]

## 1. Purpose
This procedure lets clients raise a concern about a translation, an interpreting session, billing, or conduct, and sets how Hamwatan responds, corrects, and learns from it.

## 2. How to complain
A client can complain by email, the Contact page, phone, or WhatsApp. Verbal complaints are written down by staff within one business day. A complaint does not need to be in any particular form.

## 3. Acknowledgment
Every complaint is acknowledged within one business day with a reference number and the expected next step.

## 4. Categories
- Accuracy or quality of a translation.
- Interpreting conduct, late arrival, or no-show.
- Delivery, formatting, or missing pages.
- Billing or refund.
- Confidentiality or privacy concern.
- Other.

Confidentiality and privacy complaints go straight to the Incident Response Procedure (document 08).

## 5. Investigation
1. Review the order, the source document or session record, and the assignment.
2. Ask the linguist or interpreter for their account within three business days.
3. Where needed, obtain an independent second review by a linguist not involved in the work.
4. Decide the outcome in writing.

Target: resolve within 10 business days. Complex cases are updated every 10 business days until resolved.

## 6. Corrections
- Translation errors are corrected at no charge and re-delivered in the same format.
- Where a correction changes a certified statement, a new signed accuracy statement is issued.
- Interpreting errors that affected the client are reviewed, and the client is offered a remedy the Company can justify, such as a credit or repeat session.

## 7. Refunds
Refunds follow the Refunds and Cancellations policy on the website. Where the Company made an error, it corrects the work first, and refunds the affected portion if correction is not possible.

## 8. Escalation
If the client disagrees with the outcome, they may ask for a review by the Owner. A decision is given in writing within 10 business days.

## 9. Records
Each complaint record includes the date, category, facts, investigation, outcome, corrective action, and closure date. Records are kept for three years (document 02).

## 10. Trend review
Every quarter, the Owner reviews complaint categories and counts, looks for repeated causes, and makes at least one corrective action where a pattern appears. The summary is shared with the team.
