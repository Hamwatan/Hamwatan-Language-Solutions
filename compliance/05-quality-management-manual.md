# Quality Management Manual (ISO 17100-Aligned)

Version 1.0 | Owner: [OWNER NAME] | Effective: [DATE]

This manual describes how Hamwatan plans and runs its translation and interpreting work. It is written to the structure of ISO 17100 (translation services) so that it can be audited when certification is pursued. It does not claim certification.

## 1. Scope and status
Hamwatan provides translation (certified and non-certified), interpreting (on-site, video, and phone), and related language services in Dari, Pashto, and Farsi. Certification status: not certified. Target: ISO 17100 certification after the internal audit in Section 12.

## 2. Resources
### 2.1 Personnel
- Translators and interpreters are qualified by education, training, and experience, as in document 06.
- The Owner is responsible for quality management and the assignment of linguists.

### 2.2 Technical resources
- Secure file handling (document 01), a project tracking sheet, and a terminology glossary per client where needed.

## 3. Client request and quote
1. The client submits a request with languages, document type, pages or duration, deadline, and format.
2. We check the request for feasibility, including language, dialect, deadline, and any conflicts.
3. We issue a written quote with the scope, price, and delivery date.
4. The client accepts the quote in writing. Work starts only after acceptance and any required payment step.

## 4. Assignment
- Linguists are matched by language pair, dialect, subject area, and experience.
- A linguist with a conflict of interest is not assigned.
- The assignment record names the translator and reviewer and is kept with the project.

## 5. Translation process
1. Preparation: review the source, mark unreadable parts, agree on names and terms.
2. Translation: a qualified translator translates the source.
3. Revision: a second qualified linguist checks accuracy, completeness, terminology, and names.
4. Review or proofreading: a final check for language, format, and numbers.
5. Final check: confirm the output matches the source, including dates, seals, and stamps.

For Shamsi (Solar Hijri) dates, every date is converted and checked a second time.

## 6. Interpreting process
1. Booking and scope review.
2. Interpreter assignment with a conflict check.
3. Briefing: the client shares the agenda in advance. Medical details are not placed in the booking form.
4. Session, with the interpreter working accurately, completely, and neutrally.
5. Session record: a short summary if requested. Records follow the retention schedule.

## 7. Delivery
Documents are delivered in the agreed format with a signed accuracy statement for certified work. Delivery uses secure channels only.

## 8. Feedback, complaints, and corrections
Follow the Complaints and Corrections Procedure (document 09). Errors found after delivery are corrected at no charge and recorded.

## 9. Measurement
Each month the Owner records on-time delivery rate, correction rate, complaint count, client repeat rate, and average response time. The results are reviewed and the weakest measure is given a corrective action.

## 10. Records
Project records, assignments, revision logs, complaints, and corrections are kept for the periods in document 02.

## 11. Continual improvement
Corrective actions are assigned an owner and a due date and checked for effectiveness after 90 days.

## 12. Internal audit
Before external certification, an internal audit checks this manual against practice. Findings are recorded, fixed, and re-checked. The audit is performed by someone not responsible for the area audited, or by the Owner with a documented checklist.
